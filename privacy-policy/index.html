Privacy Policy

Last updated: 25 November 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how Satish Rao ("we", "us", or "our") collects, uses, and protects information you provide when you visit asksatishrao.com (the "Site"), register for The Authority Shift masterclass, join The ABC Club, or enrol in ABC Advisory Academy.

1. Information We Collect

When you register for The Authority Shift masterclass, we collect:

  • Full name
  • WhatsApp number
  • Email address
  • Your advisory practice

When you enrol in ABC Advisory Academy, we collect your full name, email address, and mobile number (optional).

When you join The ABC Club, a WhatsApp Community, your name and WhatsApp number are visible to us and, as with any WhatsApp Community, to other members.

We also collect the page you submitted a form from, for internal tracking purposes only.

When you visit the Site, we and our third-party service providers may automatically collect:

  • Browser type, operating system, and device information
  • IP address and approximate geographic location
  • Pages visited, time spent, and referral source
  • Interactions with site content (scroll depth, clicks)

This information is collected through cookies and similar tracking technologies described in Section 4 below.

2. How We Use Your Information

We use the information we collect to:

  • Process your masterclass registration and send your seat confirmation and The Standing Gap Score Workbook
  • Add you to ABC Advisory Academy on TagMango when you enrol in a programme
  • Send you updates, resources, and direct communications from Satish Rao, including on WhatsApp
  • Understand how visitors use the Site so we can improve it
  • Measure the effectiveness of advertising (Meta Ads)
  • Comply with applicable legal obligations

We do not sell your personal information to third parties.

3. Third-Party Services

We use the following third-party services that may collect or process your data:

We use Google Analytics (via Google Tag Manager) to understand how visitors interact with the Site. Google Analytics collects anonymised usage data using cookies. This data is processed by Google LLC. You can opt out by installing the Google Analytics Opt-out Browser Add-on. For more information, see Google's Privacy Policy.

We use the Meta Pixel to measure the effectiveness of advertising on Meta platforms (Facebook and Instagram). The Pixel may collect information about your interactions with the Site and match it with your Meta account. You can manage your ad preferences at facebook.com/ads/preferences. For more information, see Meta's Privacy Policy.

The ABC Club runs as a WhatsApp Community, a service operated by Meta. Your membership and messages there are also governed by WhatsApp's own Privacy Policy.

When you enrol in ABC Advisory Academy, your name, email address, and mobile number are forwarded to TagMango, the platform that hosts the Academy. TagMango's collection and use of your data is governed by their own Privacy Policy.

We use Pabbly Connect as an intermediary to route masterclass registrations and Academy enrolments. Data passes through Pabbly's servers in transit. Pabbly does not retain your data beyond what is necessary for this routing function.

4. Cookies

We use the following types of cookies:

  • Analytics cookies - Google Analytics cookies (_ga, _gid, _gat) to understand Site usage. These are set only after the page loads and are not used to identify you personally.
  • Advertising cookies - Meta Pixel cookies (_fbp, _fbc) to measure ad campaign effectiveness.
  • Functional cookies - Session-level cookies required for the Site to function correctly. These expire when you close your browser.

You can control cookies through your browser settings. Note that disabling cookies may affect the functionality of the Site.

5. Data Retention

We retain personal information you submit through the masterclass registration or Academy enrolment for as long as your membership or enrolment is active, or as required by law. Analytics data collected via Google is retained according to Google's standard data retention settings. You may request deletion of your data at any time by contacting us.

6. Your Rights

Under applicable law, you may have the right to:

  • Access the personal data we hold about you
  • Request correction of inaccurate data
  • Request deletion of your data
  • Withdraw consent for communications at any time (by unsubscribing, or by leaving The ABC Club)

To exercise any of these rights, contact us at satish@asksatishrao.com.

7. Security

We take reasonable technical measures to protect your personal information from unauthorised access, disclosure, or misuse. However, no transmission over the internet is completely secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

8. Children's Privacy

The Site is intended for experienced financial advisors and professionals. We do not knowingly collect personal information from anyone under the age of 18.

9. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. The date at the top of this page will reflect the most recent revision. Continued use of the Site after any change constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.

10. Contact

For any questions about this Privacy Policy or how we handle your data, please contact:

satish@asksatishrao.com
